Packaging · Illustration

Mimo

Sparkling fruit soda with a face for every flavour.

Overview

Four flavours, four characters.

Mimo gives each flavour its own personality. Hand-drawn portraits for Blueberry, Orange, Strawberry and Apple sit at the centre of every label, surrounded by fruit doodles, bubbles and a hand-lettered wordmark that ties the range together across cans and glass bottles.

Role
Packaging design, illustration
Product
Sparkling fruit soda
Flavours
Blueberry, Orange, Strawberry, Apple
Formats
330 ml can, glass bottle

CharactersOne portrait per flavour — Blueberry, Orange, Strawberry and Apple.

ProcessHow the characters come to life — from loose pencil construction to flat colour, texture and final details.

LabelsEach character becomes a label, framed by fruit doodles, bubbles and the hand-lettered wordmark.

Packaging330 ml cans and glass bottles across the four flavours.

CampaignSparkling fruit soda, different flavours.
