Roomina

A meeting room scheduler identity shaped by doors, thresholds, and time.

Overview

Space, schedule, and open doors.

Roomina is a meeting room scheduling platform created to make shared workspaces and meeting spaces intuitive to book and effortless to navigate. The identity is built upon an architectural metaphor: an angled doorway with a round knob nestled into the leg of the letter ‘R’. Mirrored and interlocked, the forms create a unified mark of collaboration and open access, carrying seamlessly across bilingual lockups in English and Persian.
