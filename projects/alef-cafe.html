Alef Cafe

Hand-drawn characters woven into Persian lettering.

Overview

Five pouches, five characters.

Each Alef Coffee pouch carries its own character — a hiker, a runner, a road-tripper, a reader in an armchair and someone juggling a busy day — drawn into Persian letterforms. A six-colour palette, a flowing pattern and die-cut illustrated tags carry the identity from packaging to cups, tote bags, gift boxes, apparel and the shop window.
